About Us

X-1 is the international Class Programme of Muhammadiyah 2 Senior High School Sidoarjo. We're a group of students who share classrooms, projects, and everyday moments together. This page is our home base: a place to introduce ourselves, show what we're up to, and share how to reach us.

Our hope for this class is to become a supportive, united, and positive class where everyone feels comfortable and valued. Our vision is to create a class that is active, responsible, and respectful while growing together. Our mission is to build good communication, support each other, respect differences, and work together to achieve our goals.
